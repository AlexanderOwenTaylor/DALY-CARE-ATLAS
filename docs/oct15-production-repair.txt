DALY-CARE production repair: 15 October 2026

Approved outcome
Recover the retained October 15 aggregate outputs without another database run;
make clinical prerequisites visible before full collection; keep the single
source-73 runner, offline/hosted support and screened site.zip publication.

Loading
Exact-field tables are split at source boundaries with a 128 KiB target.
Opening a field table reads one matching shard. Next continues from the exact
returned offset; the locator still counts and searches every field identity.
The unchanged hard limits are 512 KiB for the complete automatic opening route
and 256 KiB for any shard. R and Python use the same rules as the browser.
Failed source-page budgets record the exact table/source and dependency sizes
in internal logs/loading-budget-failure.json. Atomic publication is retained.

Clinical readiness
DALYCARE_ATLAS_PREFLIGHT_ONLY=TRUE runs schema/configuration checks and bounded
finite encoding probes, without full profiling or producing a website. It
returns preflight_only=TRUE and all collection/publication phases not_started.
Normal runs also save the readiness report before profiling. Its unresolved
checks never imply absence of clinical observations.

Internal outputs
logs/clinical_readiness.csv:
  table_name,source_column,requirement,status,action
logs/person-keys.review-required.csv:
  table_name,person_key,reviewed,evidence
logs/encoding_diagnostics.csv:
  encoding,n_people,table_name,source_column,status
These files are excluded from the public manifest and sharing package.
The person-key worksheet preserves reviewed configuration and leaves guessed
schema candidates unreviewed. No arbitrary text, identifier or exact numeric
measurement is included in encoding probes. Their finite vocabulary is a
diagnostic aid, not a clinical value mapping.
An empty registry query emits empty_source with zero people. A reviewed key
remains in the worksheet even when schema is unavailable or its column absent;
the readiness status reports that mismatch without authorising publication.

Evidence limits
The October 15 archive has 134 unrecognised registry fields but no token
frequency evidence. Their clinical mappings cannot honestly be reconstructed
from outcome counts. Source-specific codebooks/finite NGC probes are required.
The 40 missing numeric unit bindings still require authoritative stored-unit
metadata and approved bands. Existing contracts and checksums are unchanged;
no historical CSV receives a new interpretation from this patch.
The additional default patient and SDS_pato person keys reuse the checked-in
high-confidence mappings. SP still requires explicit linkage review.

Terminology investigation
The pinned official IFCC snapshot defines 3,319 of 3,378 observed NPU codes.
All 59 unresolved codes are also absent from that official snapshot; none can
be recovered by copying an omitted definition from it. Keep them unresolved
until a trustworthy definition is available. No guessed names or units added.

Acceptance

actual PostgreSQL synthetic queries, production archive rebuild/reconciliation,
